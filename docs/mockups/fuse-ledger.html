Signals

waiting for you going done bold title done, unseen cheap balanced best

The tier glyph is a fuse rating: one, two or three bars. It sits beside every model name, in the ledger and the composer, so the "why" of routing is readable without a tooltip.

Words

Sentence case everywhere. No eyebrows, no taglines, no tracked capitals. Section labels say what the group is (a project name, "Yesterday", "Waiting for you"). Empty states say what is true and what to do: "Nothing running. $0.00 spent today."

Motion

One moment: when an answer finishes, its figure settles into the margin and the thread total updates. While a model works, the margin shows live tokens and cost. Nothing else moves unless you moved it.
